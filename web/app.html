What is a connection?

The assistant answers only from a database. A connection tells it which one. It reads it on every question and checks for new data every few seconds, so the answers follow your data live.

Your dataDemo data, your CSV files, a SQLite file or a PostgreSQL database. Read only: the assistant can never change it.
Live updatesWhen your pipeline adds today's prices, or you add them here, the next answer already uses them. No restart.
AI models (optional)Free or paid providers make answers more flexible. Without any, it still answers offline.

About

A private Arabic and English market assistant that runs on your computer.

It plans each question, runs safe read-only queries, and answers like an analyst: a sentence for one fact, a table and a chart for comparisons, and follow-up questions you can tap. Open “How I got this” under any answer to see the exact steps.

Your chats are saved on this computer only, in the app's folder. Delete any of them from the sidebar.

Install it as an app from your browser menu (Install / Apps). It runs only on this computer at 127.0.0.1.

Answers are information, not investment advice.
